# Description

### Bunny

**Bunny** is a cute and cuddly robot companion that players can use to assist them in battles. With large floppy ears, glowing eyes, and a small fluffy tail, it’s an adorable addition to any team—but don’t be fooled by its looks. While Dragon is the most powerful and Neko ranks second, Bunny robots surpass them both in speed, attacking and moving faster even though their damage output is lower. This speed-focused utility robot emphasizes rapid movement and efficient resource collection, offering buffs—not as strong as Kitsune’s—and the ability to gather items and deliver them to chests, though not as efficiently as Honey. Each new version of the Bunny improves on the last, maintaining a lightweight, springy design that balances agility with playful energy.
### Bunny2

**Bunny2.0** is a more advanced version of the robot-type Bunny, featuring a sleeker design and upgraded abilities. Enhanced with state-of-the-art technology, it is faster, stronger, and more intelligent than its predecessor. While Dragon remains the most powerful and Neko ranks second, Bunny robots outperform both in terms of speed, attacking and moving faster even though their damage output is lower. Bunny2.0 maintains this speed advantage, now with reinforced structure, enhanced processing systems, and improved buff capabilities—though still not as potent as Kitsune’s. Like earlier models, it can collect items and deliver them to chests, not as efficiently as Honey but steadily improving with each new iteration. Bunny2.0 represents a significant leap forward in agile, utility-focused robot companions.
### Dragon

**Dragon** robots are fearsome combat specialists engineered for frontline warfare and overwhelming force. These powerful machines feature distinctive draconic elements—wings, talons, and spiked tails—creating an intimidating presence on the battlefield. With the highest damage output of any robot type, Dragons excel at aggressive combat tactics and area control, making them ideal for players who prefer direct confrontation over subtlety. A fighting maniac, she is the most powerful among the robots; she has a heavy sword that she swings around. She is adept at hunting and patrol, and can’t perform menial tasks, but can at least pick up items from the monsters she kills.
### Honey

**Honey** is a versatile robot companion specialized in organization, automation, and beekeeping. With a distinctive hexagonal-patterned design reminiscent of a beehive, this robot excels at item sorting, inventory management, and handling bee-related tasks. More of a house worker than a fighter, Honey can sort items, transfer them in and out of chests, and manage resources in workbenches, furnaces, and other stations. She also has a particular affinity for bees, efficiently managing hives and honey production. Her hexagonal body panels and bee-like features create an instantly recognizable silhouette that perfectly reflects her specialized functions.
### Kitsune

**Kitsune** robots draw inspiration from Japanese folklore, featuring fluffy fox ears and multiple flowing tails that increase in number as they level up, up to a total of nine. These nimble companions are not combatants but rather supporter units, providing powerful buffs to players and other robot companions alike. Much like a priest, each tail unlocks new abilities, enhancing their effectiveness as they grow. Unlike combat-focused models, Kitsune prioritizes enhancement effects and team synergy.
### Neko

**Neko** is a swift, agile robot companion that ranks second only to Dragon in combat prowess. With cat-like features including large fuzzy ears, and a long tail, this feline fighter uses giant gauntlet-like claws to engage enemies up close. Though she falls behind the Dragon in raw power, she can do everything the Dragon can—plus a bit more—thanks to her superior speed and precision. Neko excels in stealth attacks and rapid strikes, making her a versatile choice for players who value both combat effectiveness and mobility in battle.
### Vanilla

**Vanilla** is the foundation of the Lovely Robot line—a friendly, approachable, and versatile robot companion designed specifically for players new to the mod. While not specialized in any particular area, Vanilla provides balanced assistance across a variety of tasks: she can fight, hunt, pick up items, store and retrieve them from chests, and patrol as needed. As a general-purpose robot, she is easy to craft and ideal for newcomers, though she is the weakest in overall capability compared to other models. Featuring a simple, clean design with a small belt pouch, Vanilla projects a welcoming and practical presence for any player starting out.
