Squad Formation

When multiples robots are deployed to follow you or when you select a squad from your robots and give them the order to follow (and protect) you they won’t randomly stumble/trip upon each other or all attack the same hostile entity, instead they will take position and shift around, taking turns and properly analysing the situation.

For example when you have about 3 robots deployed they will take a triangle shape with you in the centre, when there is four they will either take a square or losango, when only two they will either be back/front or side by side with you always on the center. The most tricky part is when moving around in formation, because the player can often enter places that is too narrow like a tunnel of 2x1 blocks, in this scenarios the robots needs to be smart and station some of their troops nearby and sent one or two scouts with the player and when they find a spacious place they rest can teleport/follow behind, this is to avoid clusters. There can be more to this but the whole point of this system is to allow players to move around with around 10 robots and not be a major headache when going through small places or dangerous places where they can fall into oblivion or such.

When moving in formation the player will often make small movements like rotate around itself or encounter places such as walls in this scenarios the robots should understand how to position themselves, instead of each one of them have one single fixed position that they always strive to be at, the position that always change should be assigned to the nearest robot of it, só if a player facing forward quickly turns left and go in that direction the robot that was moving ahead of him don’t try to go to his front but became the protector of his side, while the previous one that was on is side became the one on the front, the responsibilities shifts, à similar occurrence can happen when the player is facing a wall meaning there’s no ahead or to the sides we need to identify it and arrange appropriately!

Another thing is the robots should not all attack one hostile entity while leaving the player all defenceless, só they take turns based on several criteria’s, like how strong is the entity or how many there is to know how many to deploy and how many to still protect the player, in case there are too many they can all go to fight, só they can choose which to go fight in several manners one to allow the weakest of them to gain experience and get stronger but repeatedly send them when there’s one or weak enemies, they also access which one of them is in better shape and which isn’t to organise the formation and send them to the middle in order to protect until they get healthier again or is repaired, there are more criteria’s we can use for them to apply and best select the formation or attack.
