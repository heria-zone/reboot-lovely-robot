#### Legacy

**Bunnies**
While dragon is more powerful, and neko is second to that, the bunnies robot have higher speed compared to them, even though neko is a middle ground between power and strength the bunnies outperform her in terms of speed, attacking faster and running faster, but the damage is lower, bunnies can give out some buff but not like kitsune, they can collect items and deliver to chest, but not as efficient as honey, more like vanilla, each version of the bunny outperforming the predecessor

**Honey**
Honey is more of a house worker she can perform items sorting, taking in and out from chest, put in working benches or furnaces and such, she has an thing of herself where she likes bees and can manage bees and honey production.

**Vanilla**
She can fight, hunt, pick-up item, store them in chest, take out items from chests, patrol as she is a general purpose robot and very easy to craft as a helper for the new comers, but in downside she is the weaker ever aspect compared to others. She has a little belt pouche.

---
#### Reboot

**Dragon**
A fighting maniac, she is the most powerful among the robots, she has an heavy sword that she swings around, she is adept at hunting, patrol, and can’t perform menial tasks, but can at least pick up items from the monsters she kill

**Kitsune**
She is not a combatant but rather a supporter companion, she gives out buffs to the player and other robot alike companion, she is like a priest, which each level up, she gain more tails, up to a 9 in total, each tail unlocking something, once she reaches all the tail she will be a formidable supporter.

**Neko**
Second most powerful, using giant gauntlet like claws she fights up close to enemies, she falls behind compared to Dragon, but can do everything the dragon girl can do, plus some more.
